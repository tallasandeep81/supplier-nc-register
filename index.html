The 8D Framework (D1–D8)

Full 8-Discipline problem-solving protocol. This register actively tracks the three stages under the 2-2-2 rule (highlighted below) — D1/D2/D6/D7/D8 are carried out around those stages but aren't separately timed columns in the sheet.

StageDisciplineWhat it coversTimeline
D1Establish the TeamAssemble a cross-functional team with the knowledge, authority and time to investigate and fix the problem.—
D2Describe the ProblemDefine the non-conformance in quantifiable terms — what, where, when, how many (the Nature of Complaint / Sub-Defect fields at NC creation).—
D3Interim Containment ActionIsolate and quarantine all suspect stock across the supply chain so no more defective parts reach TVL's line. Tracked in this register.2 Days
D4Root Cause AnalysisIdentify and verify the true root cause using 5-Why, Fishbone, or similar tools. Tracked in this register.2 Weeks
D5Choose Permanent Corrective ActionSelect and verify the corrective action will resolve the problem with no undesirable side effects.—
D6Implement & ValidateRoll out the corrective action, remove interim containment, and confirm the fix holds over time.—
D7Prevent RecurrenceUpdate PFMEA, control plans, and work instructions so the same failure mode can't recur — at this part or elsewhere.—
D5–D8CAPA ClosureD5 through D8 combined — permanent fix implemented, validated, and systemised. Tracked as one closing stage in this register.2 Months
D8Congratulate the TeamRecognize the team's effort and formally close and archive the 8D record.—
